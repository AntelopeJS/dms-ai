# Activity

The Activity page (`/modules/ai/activity`) is the audit log of every tool the assistant used.

![The Activity log with its tabs and filters](../images/activity.webp)

- Tabs: **All**, **Changed files**, **Asked you**, **Denied or blocked**, **Failed**, with quick filters.
- Each row shows when, the action and its target, how it was allowed, and the result.
- **Export CSV** downloads the log.

## Action detail

Click a row to open its detail on the right (`?record=<id>`, `J` / `K` to step): the tool, how it was allowed, its result, its arguments, the **decision trail** (requested, asked you, allowed by whom, done), and the diff it produced. **Open conversation** and **View change set** jump to the context.

![The detail of a Builder "Delete page" call, approved by the user, with its diff](../images/activity-detail.webp)
